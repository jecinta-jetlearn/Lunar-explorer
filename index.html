The Exploration Continuum

Five decades of robotic pioneers and crewed landings that transformed our sister world from an untouchable beacon into a stepping stone for the cosmos.

1959 SOVIET UNION

Luna 2: First Cosmic Artifact Touchdown

  • First human-made spacecraft to impact the lunar surface in Mare Serenitatis.
  • Proved the Moon does not possess a significant planetary magnetic dipole.
  • Delivered spherical pennants and paved the way for automated lunar navigation.
1969 NASA

Apollo 11: Tranquility Base Touchdown

  • Neil Armstrong & Buzz Aldrin complete first crewed landing in Mare Tranquillitatis.
  • Returned 21.5 kg of regolith; proved humanity can live and operate on another world.
  • Deployed laser retroreflector array still measured by terrestrial observatories today.
1970 SOVIET UNION

Lunokhod 1: The First Planetary Rover

  • First remote-controlled mobile robot to operate on any extraterrestrial body.
  • Covered 10.5 km over 321 Earth days in Mare Imbrium transmitting 20,000 images.
  • Tested regolith soil mechanics using conical penetrometer and X-ray spectrometer.
2013 CNSA

Chang'e 3: Return to the Lunar Soil

  • First soft landing on the lunar surface in 37 years (since Luna 24 in 1976).
  • Yutu rover analyzed ilmenite basalt compositions in Mare Imbrium with ground radar.
  • Operated extreme-UV telescope observing Earth's plasmasphere from the Moon.
2019 CNSA

Chang'e 4: Historic Far Side Landing

  • First ever soft touchdown on the mysterious Far Side (Von Kármán crater).
  • Used Queqiao relay satellite parked at Earth-Moon L2 Lagrangian point.
  • Carried mini-biosphere cultivating first cotton plant sprout on the Moon.
2023 ISRO

Chandrayaan-3: The Polar Frontier

  • Historic first soft landing in the ultra-cold Lunar South Pole Region (69.37° S).
  • Pragyan rover spectroscopically confirmed elemental Sulfur (S) in polar regolith.
  • ChaSTE probe recorded groundbreaking soil temperature profile down to 8 cm depth.
2024 JAXA

SLIM: Pinpoint "Moon Sniper"

  • Pioneered vision-based autonomous navigation landing within 55 meters of target.
  • Deployed LEV-2 (SORA-Q) palm-sized transformable ball rover on the surface.
  • Survived extreme lunar night multiple times, far beyond original design life.
2024 CNSA

Chang'e 6: Far Side Sample Return

  • Returned 1,935.3 grams of pristine lunar soil from the South Pole-Aitken Basin.
  • Drilled 2 meters deep to collect undisturbed geologic layers from the ancient impact.
  • Provides first direct physical analysis of Far Side volcanic and crustal evolution.
2026+ NASA / GLOBAL

Artemis II & III: Crewed Polar Return

  • Artemis II flies 4 astronauts on circumlunar free-return loop aboard Orion.
  • Artemis III lands first crew at the South Pole with SpaceX Starship HLS.
  • Establishes permanent human presence, Lunar Gateway station, and Mars staging base.
